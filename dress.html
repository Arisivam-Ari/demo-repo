About Brew Haven Coffee Shop

Brew Haven Coffee Shop started in 1989 as a small roadside coffee shop. Today, we have 93 branches across India. We serve fresh coffee, tasty food, and natural Kerala tea. We provide good quality, friendly service, and affordable prices. Thank you for choosing Brew Haven Coffee Shop. ☕🌿

Our Story

Brew Haven Coffee Shop started in 1989 as a small roadside coffee shop. We served fresh coffee with care and friendly service, With the love and support of our customers, we grew step by step. Today, we are proud to have 93 branches across India. Even today, we continue to serve quality coffee, tasty food, and friendly service. Our goal is to make every customer feel happy and welcome. Thank you for being a part of our journey
